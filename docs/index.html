Secret Santa

Pre-loved presents only

There's no need to spend a cent (and no spending limit if you do). There's just one rule, and it's non-negotiable: every gift must be second-hand. Re-home a treasure from home, rescue a gem from an op shop, or hunt down something wonderful.

Everyone who RSVPs yes is automatically in the draw. No need to sign up separately.

How it works

  1. RSVP yes by Wednesday 11 November. That's it, you're in.
  2. Get your code. Once RSVPs close, names are drawn and Aleisha sends you a private code.
  3. Reveal your match. Pop your code into the box below to see who your gift is for. Keep it secret!
  4. Find their gift. Something pre-loved from home is perfect, or go treasure hunting for something second-hand. Wrap it and write their name on it (not yours!).
  5. Bring it to croquet. We'll open gifts at St Heliers Bay Croquet Club on Wednesday 2 December.

Where to find a gift

  • 🛍️ Op shops
  • 💎 A pre-loved treasure from home

  • 🔨 Trade Me
